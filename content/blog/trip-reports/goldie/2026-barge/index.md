Goldie’s BARGE 2026 TRIP REPORT

I arrived EWR in plenty of time to make the 9am flight to Vegas.  Unfortunately it was delayed 2 hours.   HOWEVER I ran into Patrick Kirwan who was on the same flight, so BARGE was off to a great start!

Adam Allknight Bachrach picked me up at the airport and took me to the Aria.  I had to stay there one night (fully comped) to take advantage of a  freeplay offer.  They even put me in a sky suite.  I got my comps ($600 in airfare refunded as freeplay) and moved to The Orleans the next day for the  remainder of the trip.

Played in the Monday charity tourney.  Finished about half way through the field.  Always great reconnecting with friends I see only once or twice a year.  

I like $20-$40 limit holdem.  Unfortunately that game never got going on Monday at Bellagio.  The poker room was noticeably quiet. Post WSOP exhaustion I was told.  But I went there on Tuesday with Adam and lost $340.  I should have never left Orleans.  I crushed the $4-$8 limit holdem game there.  In fact I didn’t have a single losing session there all week.  Net:+$281.  All cash. .

Tuesday night dinner at Oak and Fig, or Fig and Tree, or whatever it’s called was wonderful, made especially so because Mike Chow shared some of his fabulous wines with us.  Thank, Mike!!

Wednesday dinner was at Lotus of Siam.  The one at Red Rocks in Summerlin.  NolanI met us there.

Back to dinner.  I have always loved Lotus.  Their Nam Kao Tod is one of my favorite dishes EVER.  So I always look forward to getting to Lotus.  HOWEVER, while we were waiting to be seated, NolanI did nothing but bad-mouth the place.  Loudly.  “Not as good as the other location”, “Limited Menu”, “Always disappointed here”, etc., etc. We all know how opinionated NolanI is, especially about food.  And we got a full dose from him that night.

However, once the food arrived (we ordered  family style) his tune changed immediately.  NolanI couldn’t get enough.  “Wonderful”, “Delish!”, “So good”, and on and on.   He was both surprised and delighted.  So I don’t know how or why NolanI had such poor prior experiences at the Red Rocks Lotus, but I’m glad he liked it.  Dinner was great.  Just ask NolanI.

Banged around the $4-$8 games and fed the VP machines until Friday’s TOC event.  

Obviously it’s easy to say you played well when you run good.  And I ran good in the TOC, ultimately finishing 4th to Doug Grismore’s 4 outer.  But I also played well, scooping 2 important pots on stone cold bluffs. Overall I don’t think I made too many (if any) big mistakes.  So I was happy with my play.  AND I won my first-ever ADB last longer! However, I was sad to have missed the Karoke Peter celebration.  I think Matt Savage was well received.  I know he liked playing with us.

So, on to the main event.   The MOST FUN I have playing poker every year is the Go4Goldie bet.

For any newbies, first of all thank you for attending BARGE.   I hope you had a great time.  I know I enjoyed meeting several of you.  Would love to see you again at any future ARG event.  

Secondly, the Go4Goldie is a last-longer bet I’ll take with ANY other BARGEr (who plays in the tourney, heh) of up to $20, at my opponent’s choice. I always have a blast calling players over to pay me when they bust, and truth be told, I have just as much fun announcing my all-in and then going around paying off those who beat me.

 Unfortunately my Friday run-good was nowhere to be found on Saturday.   Again, I don’t think I made any big mistakes; cards just didn’t come my way.  Anyway, if you’ve read this far I know you are dying to know the results.

I had individual last-longers with 60 BARGErs, total risk of $995.  I lost $735 to 42 of you.  I don’t know how you did it, but there it is.  Congrats.  Thankfully 18 of you suck so much at poker that you  lost $265 to me, cutting my Go4Goldie loss to $465 for the event.  From the bottom of my wallet, thank you.

Saturday night was dinner at Piero’s.  I’ve known the founder Freddie for 40 years.  They had some weird and unfortunate shit happen lately so I was delighted to see it taken over by the Siegel family (Bagelmania).  The Siegels are accomplished restauranteurs and I’m delighted to report that the food was as great as ever.

I didn’t play in any reindeer games this year.  I was exhausted.  BUT, I made it on to the 7am flight to EWR and avoided delays that because common due to weather later on Sunday and Monday.

These days, after years and years and countless hours of poker I find that the dinners and just hanging out is most important to me than poker.  Even the sad moments remembering loved ones we’ve lost are special to me because our group is special to me.
